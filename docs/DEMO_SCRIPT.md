# Demo script (about 85 seconds)

The judge flow from the brief: **open the app → pick a planet → zoom into a rover → play the timeline → photo + information box → translate and Save → Gallery and Like → finish on the external link.**

**Before you start:** run `npm run serve`, open http://localhost:8000 full-screen and click the **sound** button (top right) once — browsers block audio until you click. Quick restarts: `#view=mars`, `#machine=viking-1`.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | The app opens on the solar system. Drag to spin it once. | "Since the 1960s NASA has left machines all over the solar system. Cosmic Tales lets kids fly out and meet them. Every planet stays lit from any angle." |
| 0:08 | Click the red **Mars** tab. Let the camera zoom all the way in. | "Each destination has its own colours. The camera dives in first — the cards only appear once we've arrived." |
| 0:15 | Click **Viking 1** in the bottom bar. Wait for the close-up, then the Cosmic Card. | "We zoom right down to the lander at its real landing site, Chryse Planitia, with sunlight and soft shadows. This is its Cosmic Card — facts straight from NASA's mission files." |
| 0:26 | Press **Timeline** on the card. | "The timeline opens on the right; the 3D lander stays beside it." |
| 0:30 | Press **Play story** (Astronaut voice). Let it play ~6 s, then press **→** once. | "An astronaut voice reads each moment. Photo and story share one big box — no scrolling — and every moment has its own photo; none ever repeats." |
| 0:42 | Open the **language** menu in the box, pick **हिन्दी** (Hindi), then **ಕನ್ನಡ** (Kannada), then back to **English**. | "Only the story box translates — Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu and more — while the buttons stay the same." |
| 0:50 | Press **Save**, choose *This moment* → *New collection* "My Mars trip" → **Save**. | "Kids can save a moment or a whole timeline into their own collections. The tick confirms it, and the Saved button keeps it for later." |
| 0:57 | Close the timeline. Click **Gallery** (top right). Tap the **♥** on two photos, then switch to **Liked**. | "The Gallery has every photo once. Tap the heart to like a photo, and the Liked tab keeps your favourites together." |
| 1:06 | Open a liked photo and press **→** once, then **×** twice. | "It browses like a phone gallery — here just the liked ones." |
| 1:12 | Reopen **Viking 1 → Timeline**, press **End**, and click **Open NASA Science** under *"For more information, open this link."* | "Every story ends with a link to NASA's own page, so curious kids can keep going. It opens in a new tab." Switch back to the app tab. |
| 1:20 | Click the **astronaut** (lower right), type `When did Viking 1 land?`. | "And Astro answers only from our NASA files, quoting them with the source." |

**If something is slow:** on a slow laptop the first zoom can take a moment; the card and bottom bar still appear after about 4 seconds. Liked photos and Saved items are stored in that browser — clear them before the demo if you practised (Gallery → Liked → tap the hearts; Saved → delete).
